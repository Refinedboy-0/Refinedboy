A simple skincare routine for men

You do not need a ten-step shelf to look after your skin. Start with a gentle cleanser, a moisturizer that suits your skin, and daily sun protection. Add targeted products only when you know what you want them to address.

Beginner's guide · Updated October 26, 2026 · Educational information, not a medical diagnosis

The three essentials

1. Cleanser

Use a gentle, non-abrasive face wash. Lukewarm water and your fingertips are enough; scrubbing hard can irritate skin.

2. Moisturizer

Choose a texture you will use consistently. Creams often suit drier skin; lighter lotions or gels may feel better on oilier skin.

3. Sunscreen

For daytime, choose broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. Reapply as directed, especially after swimming, sweating or extended time outdoors.

This basic routine is a useful starting point because it covers cleansing, hydration and protection. The American Academy of Dermatology recommends gentle cleansing and sun protection, and explains that products should be applied in a sensible order. See its advice on washing your face, applying skincare in order and choosing sunscreen.

Morning and evening: what order should you use?

In the morning

  1. Cleanse gently, or rinse with water if a full cleanse leaves your skin feeling dry.
  2. Apply moisturizer if your skin needs it.
  3. Finish with broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher on exposed skin.

At night

  1. Cleanse to remove sunscreen, excess oil and daily grime.
  2. If you use a treatment, apply it according to its label and introduce it gradually.
  3. Apply moisturizer to help keep skin comfortable.

More products do not automatically mean better results. If several new products are introduced at once, it becomes difficult to know which one is helping or irritating your skin.

How to choose products for your skin type

Oily or shiny skin

Try a lightweight, non-comedogenic moisturizer and a gentle cleanser. Avoid repeatedly washing or aggressively scrubbing to remove oil, since irritation can make a routine harder to tolerate.

Dry or tight skin

Look for a creamier moisturizer and a gentle cleanser that does not leave your skin feeling stripped. Applying moisturizer while the skin is slightly damp can help retain moisture.

Sensitive skin

Keep the routine simple and introduce one product at a time. Stop using a product that causes persistent burning or irritation; seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional if symptoms continue.

Combination skin

You may have oiliness in the forehead, nose and chin but dryness elsewhere. A light moisturizer across the face, with extra moisturizer only on dry areas, can be a practical starting point.

What should you add after the basics?

Choose one specific concern before buying a treatment: for example, clogged pores, uneven texture or persistent dryness. Check the active ingredients, use the product as directed and avoid starting multiple strong treatments at the same time. If you have persistent acne, a rash, pain or a skin condition, a dermatologist can help you choose appropriate treatment.

When comparing products, consider the ingredients, skin type they suit, texture, size, price per amount and whether you can use them consistently. Ratings alone do not tell you whether a product is suitable for your skin.

A sensible rule: buy the basics first, use them consistently, and add a targeted product only when you can explain what you want it to do.
